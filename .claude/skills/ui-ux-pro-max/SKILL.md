# ui-ux-pro-max skill

AI-powered design intelligence for professional UI/UX. Industry: **Beauty / Spa / Grooming**.

## Industry Rules — Beauty & Spa (Service category)
- **Trust first**: ratings, reviews, and credentials above the fold
- **Conversion-optimized**: one primary CTA (Book/WhatsApp), never buried
- **Cleanliness signals**: white/cream surfaces, generous whitespace
- **Warmth**: rounded corners, warm gold accents, human photography
- **Authority**: real customer quotes, star ratings prominent
- **Mobile-first**: 70%+ of beauty bookings happen on mobile

## Recommended UI Style
**Luxury Landing Page** — hero-centric, elegant, conversion-optimized

## Color Palette — Premium Pet Spa
| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|

| Accent | `#C9A96E` (warm gold) | Stars, highlights, badges |

## Key Effects
- Backdrop-filter glass cards
- Soft box-shadows (no harsh blacks)
- Subtle grain texture on hero/dark sections
- Diagonal clip-path between hero and content
- Staggered IntersectionObserver card entrances
- Gold star ratings with animated count

## Anti-Patterns for Beauty/Spa
- ❌ Overly clinical / cold blue palettes
- ❌ Too many CTAs on one screen
- ❌ Stock photo-only (no real client reviews)
- ❌ Dense text blocks without breathing room
- ❌ Missing mobile sticky booking button
- ❌ Hard drop shadows on white cards

## Section Structure (Conversion-Optimized)
1. Hero — Hook + primary CTA (above fold)
2. Trust strip — Rating + review count + location
3. Services — What you offer (scannable)
4. About — Story + credibility
5. Gallery — Social proof (visual)
6. Reviews — Marquee of real quotes
7. Booking CTA — Final conversion
8. Footer — Contact + links

## Pre-Delivery Checklist
- [ ] Primary CTA visible without scrolling (mobile)
- [ ] Contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1 for body text
- [ ] All images have alt text
- [ ] Phone/WhatsApp links tap-friendly (min 44px)
- [ ] Loading time < 3s (lazy images)
- [ ] No horizontal scroll on 320px viewport
